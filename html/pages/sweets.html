No Sweets Challenge

Overview

What?: For 100 days I will abstain from eating all kinds of sweets (defined below).

When?: This will run from August 18th 2026-November 26th 2026

Why?: I hate how they taste so good but have absolutely no nutritional benefit.

What counts as "sweets":

  1. All forms of ice cream
  2. All forms of sweet pastry (like donuts)
  3. All sweet baked goods (cakes, pies, muffins, etc.)
  4. Sugary drinks (soda, sweet tea, etc.)

What does not count as "sweets":

  1. Fruits
  2. Greek yogurt
  3. Zero sugar sodas

I'm interested to find out how hard this will be (I like sweets a lot).
